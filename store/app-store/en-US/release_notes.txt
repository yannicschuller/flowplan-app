New in this version:
• If your server can't be reached right now (e.g. on another Wi-Fi), the app shows "Connecting to …" right away instead of an empty page – and opens your offline copy after a few seconds
• Without an offline copy, a clear message explains what's going on, with "Try again" and "Switch profile"; the app keeps trying in the background. It now also tells you where to turn on offline use: in Flowplan under Settings → Offline & devices
• Smaller improvements and fixes
