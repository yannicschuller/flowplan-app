The first version of Flowplan for iPhone and iPad:
• Connects to app.flowplan.org or your own Flowplan instances, several of them as profiles
• Push notifications with the number of unread ones on the app icon
• Widgets with settings of their own: tasks to check off, today, favorites, counters, journal and habits, time, mentions, forms, quick capture – for administrators state, live numbers and storage
• Live Activities: timer, focus, the meeting now, imports, voice memo
• Siri, Shortcuts, Control Center and the Action button: note, timer, voice memo, focus, “What's on today?”
• Sign in with a passkey or SSO, app lock with Face ID
• Quick actions: inbox, my tasks, search, new note, voice memo
• Apple Pencil on whiteboards: pressure, eraser, double-tap and squeeze; fingers move the board
