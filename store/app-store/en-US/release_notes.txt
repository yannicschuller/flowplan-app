The first version of Flowplan for iPhone and iPad:
• Connects to app.flowplan.org or your own Flowplan instance
• Push notifications with the number of unread ones on the app icon
• Widgets for the home and lock screen: tasks due, inbox, running timer
• The running timer as Live Activity on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island
• Sign in with a passkey or SSO, app lock with Face ID
• Quick actions: inbox, my tasks, search, new page
