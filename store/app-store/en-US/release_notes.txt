New in this version:
• On iPhone and iPad, swiping from the left edge opens the sidebar over the current page instead of going back – the arrows at the top left still go back. If you prefer swiping back, change it in Settings → Appearance & language → “Swipe from the left edge” (requires Flowplan 0.9.7 on the server)
• The app offers once to save your pages on the device, so you can read and edit them without a connection. App settings show whether there's an offline copy and how old it is
• If your server can't be reached, the app shows "Connecting to …" right away instead of an empty page and opens your offline copy after a few seconds. Without a copy, it tells you where to turn on offline use
• Own certificates: instances with a self-signed certificate or a private certificate authority work when your device trusts the certificate – the app explains how to set it up
• The app's animations follow Reduce Motion and your "Animations" setting in Flowplan
• Smaller improvements and fixes
