New in this version – the widgets, redone:
• Four new widgets: Next appointment (with a countdown and "Join" for video calls), Continue reading (recent pages and library books), Key figure from a dashboard (with its history) and Deadline countdown
• Several instances: each widget can show its own profile – e.g. work and personal tasks side by side
• Colours: widgets take your workspace's accent colour – or, if you prefer, a profile colour, the system colours or Flowplan's colours
• Every profile gets a colour dot, so you tell your instances apart at a glance
• Always up to date: on iOS 26 your instance refreshes widgets right after a change (needs Flowplan 0.10.2 or later)
• Made for iOS 26: tinted and clear looks, widgets in CarPlay and on the Mac, better suggestions in the Smart Stack
• Reworked spacing, type and larger tap targets in all widgets
• Smaller improvements and fixes
