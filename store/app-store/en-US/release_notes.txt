What's new:
• Add a passkey right in the app: the iOS sign-in sheet saves it to iCloud Keychain – usable in the app, in Safari and on your other devices (needs Flowplan 0.10.2 or later)
• "Cards due" widget: choose which decks it shows
• iPad with several windows: "Switch instance" opens in the window you called it from
• If an error page answers instead of Flowplan (e.g. from a reverse proxy), the app shows a note with "Try again" and "Switch profile" instead of a dead end
• Flashcard ratings made offline are sent more reliably once the instance is reachable again
• The address field now shows a full address with https:// as an example
• Smaller improvements and fixes
